                Web pages can't send lock-screen notifications on iPhone, so let Reminders do it:

                1. Open Reminders and add “Life Ledger check-in”.
                2. Tap ⓘ, turn on Date and Time (e.g. 9:00 PM), and set Repeat to Daily.
                3. When it pops up, open Life Ledger from your Home Screen.

                Always open the app from its Home Screen icon. Safari keeps a separate copy of your data.
